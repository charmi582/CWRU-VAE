# Submission readiness review

This version is a stronger journal manuscript foundation than the previous direct English conversion, but it is not yet a final submission package.

## What has been improved

- The manuscript is now written as a formal English article instead of a direct translation.
- The central claim is reframed around strict generalization validation, Paderborn external validation, threshold calibration, fuzzy health index and reproducibility.
- Federated learning is excluded from the main paper and left for future work.
- The workflow figure has been redesigned in English as a journal-style pipeline schematic.
- The figure count has been increased to nine, with each figure tied to a research question.
- The table count has been increased to eight, including literature positioning, protocol summary, CWRU results, Paderborn results, calibration policies, fuzzy health, efficiency and reproducibility.
- The discussion now explains why Paderborn results are less clean than CWRU and why that is scientifically valuable.
- Section and subsection transitions are included so the paper does not read like a project checklist.

## What still needs attention before real submission

- Several 2024--2026 references need exact author names, issue numbers and DOI verification.
- The target journal must be chosen before final formatting because reference style, section layout and figure placement differ.
- The English should receive one more human academic polish pass after all numerical values are finalized.
- If the target journal expects stronger novelty, one additional experiment on adaptive threshold calibration or plant-specific calibration could help.
- Figure captions should be adapted to the chosen journal's preferred style.

## Honest acceptance assessment

With the current positioning, the paper is more credible as an applied workflow and evaluation paper than as a novel deep learning architecture paper. It has a reasonable path for a Q3 condition-monitoring or applied engineering journal if the reference list is fully verified and the narrative remains honest about Paderborn difficulty. It would still be risky for a Q1/Q2 AI journal because the algorithmic novelty is limited.
